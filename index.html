01 / News

Resilience of the Swiss
healthcare system

    On September 16, 2026, two «Blind Spots» fishbowl discussions were held in Bern to explore how health resilience can be better integrated into everyday life in Switzerland. Participants called for:

  1. social relationships to be strengthened as part of preventive care
  2. greater emphasis on prevention and appropiate care
  3. effective antibiotics to be preserved and the development of new ones to be facilitated
  4. the security of supply to be strengthened through safe processes
  5. the public to be engaged in a nuanced manner and for long-term support to be provided

03 / About

About

«Blind Spots» is a series of events that brings together experts from science, economy and society to discuss potential crisis situations in Switzerland and to identify blind spots in the current state of knowledge. The series is sponsored by the Mercator Foundation Switzerland, the ETH Board and economiesuisse, and organized in collaboration with the Swiss Academies and the Swiss National Science Foundation.
